# text translation: points for manual review

All of `text_l_english.yml` (8,042 entries) is translated and merged into [ca_text_l_english.yml](../localisation/replace/ca_text_l_english.yml). As with [EU4.md](EU4.md), **the Catalan shown is what the file contains now**. Write your decision in the last column (or tell me) and I'll apply it.

The translators were given the conventions from [EU4.md](EU4.md) §1–3 and followed them, so this file only lists **new** questions. The open EU4 decisions (his/her helper, ruler gender, names inside `$VARIABLES$`, masculine `$ADJ$`) still apply here too.

## 1. New project-wide decisions

1. **Ordinal suffixes (`ST`/`ND`/`RD`/`TH`)** are the most important new question. The engine appends them to numbers in army, fleet, regiment and war names, and they are now masculine «r / n / r / è» (*1r exèrcit*, *2n regiment*). Known errors: 4th → «4è» (should be *4t*), 21st → «21r» (should be *21è*), and feminine nouns get a masculine ordinal (*1r bandera*, *2n Guerra*). Because of this, `NAVY_NAME` uses masculine **Estol** instead of the glossary's *Flota*, and several war names were built around masculine nouns (*Conflicte*). Options: (a) keep this; (b) make all four «a» and reword the masculine templates; (c) drop the suffix (keys empty) and write «núm. $NUM$» or «$NUM$».
2. **War names** (`*_WAR_NAME`, here and in EU4) combine `$ORDER$`, a masculine `$FIRST$` adjective and a noun. The two files currently use three patterns: «$ORDER$Conflicte $FIRST$-$SECOND$», «$ORDER$Guerra Religiosa $FIRST$-$SECOND$» (gender clash) and «$ORDER$Guerra Revolucionària ($FIRST$)». This is tied to decision 1.
3. **Trigger lines** (about 150 keys such as *Have less than*, *Is*, *Do NOT have*) are written in the 3rd person present: «Té menys de», «És», «NO té». A few EU4 entries use the infinitive («Tenir una devoció…»). Pick one form.
4. **Object pronouns:** `PRONOUN_HIM/HER` are now «ell/ella», while `EV_HIM`/`EV_HERM` in EU4 are «el/la». This belongs to the same decision as the EU4 his/her helper.
5. **Religion and heresy names:** religions follow the glossary (a mix of adjectives like *catòlica* and nouns like *xintoisme*). Heresies were written as plural nouns for their followers (*bogomils*, *valdesos*, *vells creients*) so they read well in «Heretges ($HERETIC$)». Confirm this before the religion file is done.
6. **Culture names** (about 300 keys) are now lowercase feminine adjectives (*albanesa*, *francesa*, *moscovita*), as decided in EU4 §1.4.
7. **Dates inserted after a preposition:** some texts say «fins al dia $DATE$» (to avoid «fins al 1 de…» instead of «l'1»), while others keep «el $DATE$». The difference is minor.
8. **Decision titles inside a sentence:** `bg_decision_taken` produces «va decidir Fer de Constantinoble la capital», because decision titles start with a capital letter. This can't be fixed without lowercasing every decision title.
9. **Metròpoli** is used both for the Orthodox see (`theocratic_government_orthodox`) and for a colony's motherland (EU4). The alternative for the church sense is *Metropolia*.

## 3. Changes I made for consistency after merging

Applied to both `ca_text` and `ca_EU4`:

- **Shift key** → **Maj** everywhere (8 entries said *MAJ*; core, the loading tips and `SHIFT_CLICK` already said *Maj*). The EU4 review's term table is updated to match.
- **Khan** → **Kan** in `steppe_horde_ruler*`, matching the other steppe ruler titles. (*Genguis Khan*, a person's name, is unchanged.)
- **Infamy** as a UI label → **Expansió agressiva** (`DIPRELCH_BADBOY`, EU4 `HREVOTE_IMP_AUTH`); flavour text saying «a costa d'infàmia» is unchanged.
- **Test Act** → **Llei del Test** (`the_test_act`, `test_act_title`), matching EU4 `bill_of_test`.
- **Carrack** → **Carraca** (`carrack`, EU4 `early_carrack`); the descriptions already said *carraca*, which is the DIEC form.
- **Highlander** → **de les Terres Altes** (`scottish_highlander`, its description, `braveheart`).
- **Swivel gun** → **Canó giratori** (`swivel_cannon`, `defensive_swivel_cannon`, `durrani_swivel`); the name had been *Canó de pedrer*, which clashed with the separate *Pedrer* unit.
- `support_enuchs_faction_title` → **Donar suport a les Oficines del Comerç Marítim** (had a different faction name).
- **Settlers as population** → **pobladors** in EU4 `SIB_FRONTIER_TOOLTIP` and `PAPAL_GRANT_ADVISE`; narrative event texts keep *colons*.
