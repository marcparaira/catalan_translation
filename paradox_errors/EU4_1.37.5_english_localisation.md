# EU4 1.37.5: errors in English localisation

While translating Europa Universalis IV 1.37.5.0 into Catalan, we found 15 factual errors in the country start-screen texts (`localisation/startup_screen_l_english.yml`), 11 typos and 2 text bugs. Each row gives the localisation key, the current English and a suggested fix.

## Factual errors

All in `startup_screen_l_english.yml`, the texts shown when a country is selected at the 1444 start. Most are plain errors of people, dates or direction; the Bohemia and Arabia ones change the meaning of the sentence.

| Key | Current English | What is correct | Suggested English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
